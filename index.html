IRB Consideration — Does this project need ethics approval?
Required reading before the project begins
What is IRB? The Institutional Review Board (IRB) is the university body that reviews research involving human subjects to ensure it is conducted ethically. The faculty lead may be required to obtain IRB approval before collecting any data from students — even surveys.
Projects that research teaching and learning require IRB approval, even if many are deemed exempt. If the project plans to collect, analyze, or publish findings from student data (surveys, grades, work samples, interviews), IRB review is required. The faculty lead will need to complete CITI Training and receive certification as well. Starting data collection before approval can invalidate the findings entirely and put the faculty lead and their students at risk.

Media production involves real costs and lead time. Budget for this early — production support, licensing stock footage or music, accessibility requirements (captions, audio descriptions) can all add up. Factor this into the timeline.

If the project needs data analysis support, plan for this well in advance. Student Workers
Please remind your faculty team:
  • Hiring and onboarding takes 4–6 weeks minimum — someone will need to post the position, conduct interviews, and onboard the student before they can begin paid work.
  • If this project depends on student worker contributions early in the semester, begin this process before the semester starts.
  • Confirm with the department whether a stipend or hourly employment is more appropriate for this project.
Adds a hiring task to Milestone 1 with this deadline. If the proposal includes a cost for this position, add it as a line item in Proposed Budget Line Items above.
Time & Availability
Be specific. A realistic picture of availability is essential for building a workable timeline. If team members are on 9-month contracts and the award does not include summer stipends, project work may need to pause or slow significantly during summer months.
